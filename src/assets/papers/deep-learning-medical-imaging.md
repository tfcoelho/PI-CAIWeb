# Deep Learning in Medical Imaging: A Comprehensive Survey

## Abstract
This survey provides a comprehensive overview of deep learning applications in medical imaging, covering recent advances, architectural innovations, and clinical implementations. We analyze over 200 papers published in the last five years to provide insights into current trends and future directions.

## Introduction
Deep learning has transformed medical imaging analysis, offering unprecedented accuracy in various diagnostic tasks. This survey examines the current state and future potential of these technologies.

### Convolutional Neural Networks

### Transformer-based Models
- Vision transformers

- Hybrid architectures

### Generative Models
- GANs for image synthesis
- Diffusion models
- Style transfer applications

## Conclusion
Deep learning continues to advance medical imaging capabilities, but successful clinical implementation requires addressing technical and practical challenges.
