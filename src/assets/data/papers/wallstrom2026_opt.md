# Local Radiologists, Expert Readers, or Artificial Intelligence for Prostate MRI Interpretation in Organised Prostate Cancer Testing: Evaluation of Inter-reader Variability and Cancer Detection

## Abstract

### Background and objective

This study evaluates how local radiologists, centralised expert readers, and AI models compare in interpreting prostate MRI within Sweden's organised prostate cancer testing (OPT) programme, run across the Västra Götaland and Skåne regions. It aims to characterise inter-reader variability and assess the diagnostic performance of several CE-marked and experimental AI models for detecting clinically significant prostate cancer in this population-based testing setting.
