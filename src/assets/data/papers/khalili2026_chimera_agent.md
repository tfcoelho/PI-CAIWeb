# CHIMERA-Agent Challenge: Agentic Multimodal Reasoning for Prostate Cancer Diagnosis and Prognosis

## Abstract

### Background and objective

CHIMERA-Agent extends the CHIMERA benchmark toward agentic multimodal reasoning across the prostate cancer patient journey. Rather than assuming clean, complete, single-modality data, the challenge reflects real-world urological practice across three clinical tasks: an MRI-only decision on biopsy necessity, post-biopsy risk stratification into one of four EAU/NCCN-aligned management strategies, and post-prostatectomy biochemical recurrence prediction. Submissions must provide structured reasoning traces showing how the available multimodal evidence was interpreted and integrated, with unsupported or hallucinated findings penalized; organizers supply pretrained, modality-specific tools (MRI segmentation, automated Gleason grading, csPCa detection) rather than requiring participants to train models from scratch.
